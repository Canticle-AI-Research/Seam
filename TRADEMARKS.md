# Canticle and SEAM trademark policy

This interim policy covers the Canticle, SEAM, MIRL, HS/1, SEAM-U, Canticle
Core, and Ghost names, logos, mascots, protocol identifiers, visual identity,
and trade dress (the **Canticle marks**). Until a company exists and the
relevant rights are assigned, Nicholas Thomas is the identified owner.

Software and documentation licenses do not grant trademark rights. Subject to
applicable law, truthful nominative references to an official product or
compatibility are permitted. Without separate written permission, do not:

- name a company, product, service, domain, package, or account in a way that
  suggests official status or endorsement;
- use the Canticle marks to brand modified forks or competing products;
- alter logos, mascots, or trade dress and present them as official; or
- use the marks deceptively or unlawfully.

Forks should use independent names and branding. This engineering policy is
not a registration, legal opinion, or waiver. Trademark counsel should review
and replace or ratify it after company formation and assignment.
