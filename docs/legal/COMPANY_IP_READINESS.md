# SEAM company IP readiness

This is the operational checklist for transferring the founder-owned SEAM
portfolio into a company without blurring ownership or release history.

## Before formation

- Inventory every SEAM repository, published package, private release, domain,
  mark, specification, dataset, benchmark, model artifact, design, invention,
  vendor account, and contributor.
- Record exact current ownership and third-party provenance for each item.
- Keep Nicholas Thomas as the owner/licensor in current notices.
- Preserve historical Apache and BUSL artifacts, manifests, hashes, and terms.
- Keep unsolicited external contributions paused.
- Have counsel review the proprietary license, Shield line-of-business notices,
  contribution terms, historical grants, and trademark policy.

## At formation

Execute written documents covering, as applicable:

- founder stock/equity and governance;
- founder-to-company IP assignment with a precise asset schedule;
- board/member acceptance of the assignment;
- employee and contractor invention/confidentiality agreements;
- contributor agreement with commercial-relicensing rights;
- trademark ownership and filing strategy; and
- transfers of domains, repositories, package registries, cloud accounts,
  signing keys, and vendor contracts.

The IP schedule should explicitly name SEAM, MIRL, HS/1, the SEAM SDK/client/
Node/runtime, planned SEAM-U, Ghost integrations, Canticle Core integrations,
specifications, documentation, research, models, data the founder owns, and
associated names and goodwill.

## After assignment

- Update notices prospectively to the exact company legal name; do not backdate
  or rewrite historical authorship records.
- Store signed assignments and approvals in the corporate record book, not the
  source repository.
- Move organization, registry, domain, cloud billing, signing, and recovery
  authority to company-controlled identities with hardware-backed MFA.
- Establish `legal@`, `privacy@`, `security@`, and `licensing@canticle.cc`
  responsibility and signing authority.
- Adopt customer terms, privacy/data processing terms, security commitments,
  service levels, export/sanctions review, and incident processes before launch.

## Model and data readiness

Before training or distributing SEAM-U, establish provenance and permitted use
for every training/evaluation source; ownership for code, weights, adapters,
tokenizer, prompts, annotations, and evaluations; privacy, consent, retention,
deletion, safety, and export rules; and an explicit API-only, controlled-weight,
or public-weight decision. A software license does not automatically govern
model weights.

## Counsel packet

Provide counsel a bounded packet containing the licensing matrix, repository
and contributor inventory, current/proposed licenses, founder IP schedule,
historical published grants, third-party dependency/model/data inventory,
revenue surfaces, hosted/self-host architecture, privacy/data flows, and the
questions requiring jurisdiction-specific advice.
